Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vaultex work?
Vaultex connects three types of users: Sellers upload items to the Vault (universal inventory), Listers create attractive listings with pricing, and Buyers browse and purchase items. Create an account to get started!
What is The Vault?
The Vault is our universal inventory system. Anyone can browse it, and logged-in users can upload items. Once uploaded, items can be listed by any user to sell to buyers.
Do I need an account?
You can browse The Vault and Buying pages without an account. However, to upload items, create listings, make purchases, or manage your seller dashboard, you'll need to create a free account.
How do purchases work?
When you find an item you want to buy, click "Buy Now" and submit a purchase request. The original seller will review your request and can accept or decline. Once accepted, you can leave a review.
Can I filter items?
Yes! Both The Vault and Buying pages have powerful filter options. You can search by description, filter by seller/lister, and on the Buying page you can filter by price range as well.
